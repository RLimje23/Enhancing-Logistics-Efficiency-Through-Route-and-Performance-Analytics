# Enhancing-Logistics-Efficiency-Through-Route-and-Performance-Analytics
## Company: SwiftHaul Logistics Pvt. Ltd.
## Background:
SwiftHaul Logistics is a mid-sized freight transportation company operating across major cities in India. The company owns a fleet of 50+ trucks and delivers cargo between key metro and tier-2 cities. Although the company uses GPS-based tracking, it lacks a consolidated data-driven approach to measure and optimize route performance, fuel consumption, and delivery reliability.

## Problem Statement:
Management has observed an increase in delivery delays, irregular fuel usage, and inconsistent route adherence. These inefficiencies are affecting client satisfaction, increasing operational costs, and straining fleet management.

## Objectives:
- Analyze logistics performance KPIs to identify inefficiencies.
- Determine the root causes of delivery delays.
- Evaluate the impact of weather, traffic, and route choice on key outcomes.
- Recommend strategies for route optimization, driver performance tracking, and fuel efficiency.

## Insights You Can Derive:
- Correlation between fuel consumption and traffic/weather conditions.
- Routes that frequently have late deliveries or high fuel usage.
- Driver or vehicle performance analysis based on delivery timeliness and distance accuracy.
- Identifying high-risk or inefficient delivery corridors.

## Potential Recommendations:
- Use predictive analytics to forecast delays based on historical weather and traffic data.
- Re-assign routes to drivers based on performance trends.
- Adopt fuel monitoring systems and incentivize efficient driving.
- Plan buffer times for specific cities or under known weather patterns.
